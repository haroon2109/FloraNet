# BRICS Indigenous Seed Network & Fall Armyworm Management
Seed Catalog: Drought-resilient Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) and Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum) are vital for the semi-arid regions of South Africa (ARC) and India (ICAR). 
Pest Management: To combat the transboundary Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda), ARC recommends integrated pest management using bio-traps, push-pull intercropping (e.g., using Desmodium to repel and Napier grass to trap the moth), and early-warning monitoring systems.
