# Companion Planting & Botanical Prevention (ICAR / Embrapa / ARC)
Companion planting is a preventative, non-chemical strategy: aromatic or
antagonist plants are arranged with the main crop to suppress pests and
soil-borne pathogens before infection, rather than treating after outbreak.

## Marigold for nematode control (ICAR nematology / Embrapa)
French marigold (Tagetes patula) intercropped or border-planted with tomato,
brinjal, okra, chili and cucurbits suppresses root-knot nematodes
(Meloidogyne spp.). Marigold roots produce α-terthienyl, which is nematicidal,
and the crop acts as a trap crop that breaks the nematode's recurring
season-over-season infection cycle. Published intercrop trials report large
reductions in nematode soil populations and protected yields versus
monoculture; magnitude varies with soil type and region.

## Basil as a repellent companion
Basil (Ocimum basilicum) interplanted with tomato and pepper repels thrips and
whitefly through its volatile oils and attracts pollinators and beneficial
insects, lowering recurring vector pressure without any residue.

## Legume intercrops (ICAR rotations)
Cowpea, green gram or fodder legumes intercropped with cereals (2:1 cereal:legume
arrangement) fix 40–50 kg N/acre, reduce synthetic nitrogen demand, and build
residue biomass that raises soil organic carbon and microbial resilience
against soil-borne diseases between seasons.

## Brassica biofumigant covers (Embrapa)
Mustard and radish cover crops incorporated at flowering release
glucosinolates — a natural biofumigant against soil-borne pathogens. Radish
taproots bio-drill compacted subsoil layers to 1.2 m in the Cerrado protocol,
improving subsoil nutrient uptake for the following main crop.

## Sunn hemp (Crotalaria juncea)
Sunn hemp cover suppresses root-knot and reniform nematodes while producing
high green-manure biomass incorporated 45–60 days after sowing, combining
pest prevention with soil organic carbon building in one cycle.

## General rules
- Diversify at least 2 families in the same block to interrupt specialist
  pest cycles; a 4-year break is advised for Sclerotinia-prone fields (VNIIEA).
- Prefer dusk applications for botanical sprays (neem) to protect pollinators.
- Companion strategies prevent; they do not replace scouting. Combine with
  pheromone traps (fall armyworm, every 200 m from June 15 per ICAR) and
  sentinel plots (soybean rust, Embrapa).
