# Plantio Direto / Direct Planting System (Brazil/Embrapa)
Plantio Direto is a zero-tillage agricultural system pioneered in the Brazilian Cerrado by Embrapa.
Mechanism: It relies on maintaining permanent soil cover with crop residues and utilizing biological nitrogen fixation via Bradyrhizobium inoculation.
Rotation: Often involves integrating soybean with cover crops like Brachiaria or Crotalaria to break pest cycles, improve water infiltration, and rapidly build Soil Organic Carbon (SOC).
