# Jeevamrutha Application (India/ICAR)
Jeevamrutha is an organic bio-fertilizer and soil microbiome enhancer heavily promoted by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).
Ingredients: Cow dung, cow urine, jaggery, pulse flour, and virgin soil.
Application: It promotes biological nitrogen fixation and solubilizes phosphorus. For drought-tolerant millets in the Deccan Plateau, apply 200 liters of Jeevamrutha per acre via irrigation or foliar spray every 15 days to enhance crop resilience and restore degraded soil carbon.
