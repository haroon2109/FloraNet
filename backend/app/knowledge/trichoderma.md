# Trichoderma Viride and Pseudomonas fluorescens (India/ICAR)
These are bio-fungicides and bio-control agents crucial for organic farming.
Usage: Used to treat seeds or as a foliar spray to combat wilt, root rot, and leaf blight.
Application: Mix 5-10g per liter of water for foliar spray. For soil application, mix 2kg of Trichoderma with 50kg of farmyard manure and leave for 7 days before applying to the field.
